# Delivery issue list

[Acceptance](../docs/acceptance.md) retains the final requirements. [Plan](plan.md) defines four whole-system rounds. This is the single active issue list, not a record of every past edit. Update or close an entry as evidence changes; preserve raw results in their original evidence directory.

Severity is based on consequences: **critical** is incorrect success, corruption, unauthorized access or broken commit order; **high** blocks normal use/recovery or exhausts normal resources; **qualification** is missing proof; **optimization** is a measured or suspected performance gap. Missing proof is not proof of a defect. A later-round assignment must be revisited if normal-use consequences emerge.
